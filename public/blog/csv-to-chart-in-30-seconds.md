## What You Need

A CSV file. That's it.

Any CSV works. Sales data. Survey results. Website analytics. Whatever you've got sitting in your downloads folder.

Don't have one handy? No problem. Open Excel or Google Sheets. Type some data. File > Download as CSV.

Done.

## Step 1: Open Vizor (5 seconds)

Go to the Vizor app. It loads instantly. No login screen. No tutorial popup. Just a blank canvas ready for your data.

See that big upload area? That's where we're going.

## Step 2: Drop Your File (3 seconds)

Drag your CSV file from your folder. Drop it on the upload area.

Or click to browse and select it. Either way works.

The file uploads. Vizor reads it. Your data appears in a preview table.

## Step 3: Pick Your Chart (2 seconds)

Look at the chart type selector on the left. You see icons for bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, scatter plots.

Click one.

The chart appears instantly. Your data is now visual.

That's it. You're done. Check your timer.

## Step 4: Make It Look Better (15 seconds)

The default chart works. But let's make it better.

**Change colors.** Click the color picker. Choose your brand colors or pick from the preset palettes.

**Add a title.** Click "Add Title" at the top. Type what this chart shows. "Monthly Revenue 2024" or "Customer Satisfaction Scores" or whatever fits.

**Clean up labels.** Click any axis label to edit it. Make them clear and readable.

**Adjust the legend.** Move it. Remove it. Do what makes the chart clearer.

Every change appears immediately. No render time. No loading spinner. Just instant updates.

## Step 5: Export or Share (5 seconds)

Click the export button in the top right.

Choose your format. PNG for slides. SVG for design work. PDF for reports.

Click download. The file saves to your computer.

Or click "Share" to get a link. Send it to anyone. They don't need an account to view it.
